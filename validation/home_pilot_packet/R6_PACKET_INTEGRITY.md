# R6 packet integrity

R6 is a new operator-packet identity created for one determinism remediation.
It does not rewrite R3, R4 or R5, and every earlier manifest is preserved.

R6 uses `SHA256SUMS_R6.txt`, generated by `helpers/build_r6_packet_manifest.py`.

## Why R6 exists

The R5 replay decomposition established genuine nondeterminism in the frozen
pipeline: `plane_ransac` sampled its 3-point hypotheses from the global NumPy
RNG with no seed, so the same session produced a different plane on each run.
That propagated into ROI pixels, extracted frames and `frames_mask`, half the
scalars, PCA score arrays, and finally the applied syllable label arrays.

The fix lives in moseq2-extract, not here: commit
`2c9cd86571bcc23ad6870e4da344e0f558f3f54c` on `fix/r1-ransac-determinism-r1`,
branched from the frozen `e7f585104ba25b66e5326c88c77a47e33db95635`. It samples
from a local `np.random.RandomState(0)`, touches no scientific parameter, and
bumps the output policy `roi_plane_fit` to
`lstsq-inlier-refit-deterministic-rng0`.

This packet binds that commit through `helpers/verify_r1_runtime_identity.py`,
which fails closed if the running source does not match.

## Replay comparator corrections

R5's comparison also carried defects of its own, corrected here without
relaxing scientific equality:

- the generated extraction UUID (a fresh `uuid4` per extraction) is
  canonicalised per side as rule C2 — only that exact value, only on the side
  that produced it;
- `model-applied-heldout.p` is loaded with joblib, since moseq2-model writes a
  zlib-compressed joblib container and raw `pickle.load` cannot read it;
- TIFFs are compared as decoded pixel arrays, never container bytes;
- `depth_range_diagnostic.png` is diagnostic-only: its presence stays mandatory
  through the inventory rule, its bytes are not treated as science;
- the stage-02 duplicate ROI/background TIFFs are compared as decoded pixels.

Scientific arrays and labels remain exact, tolerance remains zero, the world
remains closed, and C1 own-root canonicalisation is unchanged.
